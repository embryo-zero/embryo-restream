# Embryo Restream

Embryo Restream is Embryo-Zero's fork of [datarhei Restreamer](https://github.com/datarhei/restreamer) (fd12aee, commit fd12aee). It does sends one stream to many sites.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
